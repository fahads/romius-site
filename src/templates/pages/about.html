About RoMIUS Urology

We're dedicated to empowering your growth journey every step.

Founded in 2005

by Drs. Ronney Abaza, Mirza Baig, and Saleem Zafar, RoMIUS Urology was built on deep Northwest Ohio roots and a shared vision of elevating urologic care across the region. As pioneers of robotic surgery in Northwest Ohio, the founders were among the earliest adopters of robotic techniques in the Midwest and went on to create some of the most advanced and robust robotic surgery programs in the region. Their leadership has extended far beyond the operating room—they have served as distinguished surgical educators for the University of Toledo urology residency program, training generations of future urologists.

RoMIUS Urology is committed to meeting the urologic needs of communities throughout Northwest and West Central Ohio by recruiting highly skilled providers, investing in cutting-edge technology, and maintaining a culture of compassionate, patient-centered care. Our board-certified physicians and clinical staff span nearly every discipline within urology, allowing us to offer comprehensive services ranging from men’s and women’s health to advanced cancer care, stone disease management, minimally invasive surgery, and state-of-the-art robotic procedures.

With 17 Locations across the region,

RoMIUS Urology provides convenient access to modern diagnostics and treatment options. Our sites incorporate on-site testing and imaging services, streamlined electronic medical records, and coordinated scheduling so that patients can be seen at any of our locations with seamless continuity of care.

In addition to clinical excellence, RoMIUS Urology remains dedicated to education, innovation, and community partnerships. Our physicians actively participate in continuing medical education, mentor trainees from the University of Toledo, and collaborate with hospitals across Northwest and West Central Ohio to expand access to high-quality specialty care.

RoMIUS Urology is proud to continue expanding its footprint to serve patients with accessible, leading-edge urologic care—delivered with the expertise of a regional center and the compassion of a local practice.
